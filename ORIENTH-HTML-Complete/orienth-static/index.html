Our Origins

From Earth to Memory

At 5,400 meters, time moves slowly. Tashi, a stone gatherer, kneels on frozen ground at dawn. He removes his gloves and presses his bare hands to the ice — an old ritual his grandfather taught him.

“Before you take,” he says, “you ask the mountain. The mountain doesn't give you stone — it gives what it's ready to let go.”
